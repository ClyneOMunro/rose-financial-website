Loss of a Loved One

When a spouse dies, the surviving spouse has to process their grief while simultaneously going through a mountain of paperwork. Their struggle can be compounded exponentially if they don't have access to accounts, logins, multi-factor authentication, or delays in benefits payouts. This kit exists to prepare your family for the worst day of their lives: what genuinely needs doing in the first weeks, what the survivor benefit systems actually provide, and how to prioritize everything else.

The kit · 6 articles

This kit can help you if:
you just lost your spouse or you want to prepare your family.

If you are going through this now, start with the first-weeks checklist and let everything else wait until you need it. If you are here because you handle the money and you want your family never to face this unprepared, start with The Conversation Before. The kit is the same, but it serves two purposes: it guides a survivor, and helps everyone else.

Whose benefits are you sorting out?

The checklist, the readiness conversation, and the guidance on timing all apply to any family, whatever the employer. A dedicated private-sector benefits guide (Social Security survivor benefits, private retirement accounts, employer life insurance) is on the way; for now these universal pages will serve you well.

Why this kit exists

This kit was built for real families the practice has walked alongside after a sudden loss. In every case, the hardest part was not any single benefit. It was not knowing what needed attention now and what could safely wait. These pages are the answer to that question, written down so no one has to figure it out alone at the worst possible time.
